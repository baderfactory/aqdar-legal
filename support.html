Support

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. The app can keep your logs without an internet connection.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your device. Local data is removed with it.

Why would it ask for notifications?

Optionally, and only for prayer reminders if you enable them. The app does not send smoking alerts from an external server.
